How It Works

Voice → Intent → Agent → Space → Results
  • Step 1You speak a command in German or English
  • Step 25-step enhancement pipeline corrects speech, resolves context, clarifies ambiguity
  • Step 3Hybrid 5-tier router selects the optimal space — from keyword matching to LLM reasoning
  • Step 4Specialist agent executes the task using domain-specific tools
  • Step 5Results appear as 3D bubbles in your Multiverse workspace — indexed in the knowledge graph
