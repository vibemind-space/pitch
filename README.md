# VibeMind

### Speak ideas into existence. Let AI agents handle the rest.

VibeMind is an **open-source, voice-first AI operating system** with 14 coordinated workspaces that turn spoken ideas into fully functional software, designs, and business assets — autonomously.

> **We're raising a seed round.** If you're interested in the future of human-computer interaction, read on.

---

## The Problem

PC interaction hasn't fundamentally changed in 40 years. We still click, type, drag. Meanwhile AI can write code, design interfaces, manage projects — but every tool is isolated, every agent works alone.

## Our Solution

A unified AI workspace where **your voice is the interface**:

- **Ideas Space** — Speak a thought, get structured plans with market research
- **Project Space** — Describe an app, get full-stack code with live preview
- **Desktop Space & Rachel** — Voice-controlled OS with a personal AI assistant
- **The Brain** — 43 neuroscience-inspired modules with 14 cognitive agents
- **SWE Design & Rowboat** — From requirements to architecture to knowledge
- **Agent Farm, Video Studio & More** — The complete autonomous workspace

**86 agent modules** in a Society of Mind architecture. **5-ring radial attention network** with thalamic gating. **77% routing accuracy** and continuously learning.
